## Evidence and limits

The machine-readable audit ledger is `catalogue/gap-review.json`. It records initial missing fields, added stages and sources, original-content hashes, scoped source verification, expected checks and unresolved claims for each entry. `catalogue/sources.json` records the source URLs, contributions and review dates.

Independent review tightened the NASA verification-matrix contribution to the shall requirements included in that matrix. The Atlas requirement-status convention still also permits proposed and unresolved statements. Older price, billing, product-preview and model-availability claims were not refreshed merely because a workflow mapping was added.

Browser, build and catalogue preservation checks validate this implementation. They do not execute the hundreds of project-specific checks described by the guides or establish comparative effectiveness.
